Here you can visualize the learning dynamics of a shallow network with three neurons, each reading its own input, no biases, and monomial activation σ(t) = tk:

f(x) = a1(w1x1)k + a2(w2x2)k + a3(w3x3)k = c1x1k + c2x2k + c3x3k,

with ci = aiwik. The network is trained gradient descent on Σ(ci − ci*)2 to a random target ◆ = (c1*, c2*, c3*), optionally plus λ times the squared norm of the weights. Initialization □ is random, with controllable scale σ.

Weights: more opaque when larger, dashed when negative. Red: active neurons.
